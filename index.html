Tidewatch latest forecasts and hindcasts

Copied from Tidewatch every 6 hours; updated 2026-10-31 12:25 UTC. Links keep the same address and are overwritten each update.

CBBT, MNPT (Money Point), DAHL (Dahlgren) and WADC (Washington DC) are built here from NOAA CO-OPS 6-minute observations, because Tidewatch's records for them are stale (CBBT, MNPT since May 2022) or come from another gauge (DAHL, WADC): a 27-constituent harmonic tide referenced to m30, and a Tidewatch-style 36-hour forecast (tide plus 0.7 times the NWS P-ETSS mean surge, offset to the latest residual). NAVD88 at CBBT and Money Point is converted from MLLW with NOAA VDatum (NAVD88 zero 1.92 and 1.77 ft above MLLW).

BRDH (Back River at Dandy Haven), a Classic Tidewatch station missing from the current Tidewatch, is built the same way from the VIMS radar gauge's 1-minute record on the VIMS THREDDS server (MLLW 9.71 ft below the gauge), with Sewells Point's P-ETSS surge and NAVD88 from NOAA VDatum (1.63 ft above MLLW).

USLN-X is the experimental FloodVISION-AI webcam water level at Little Neck Creek at Pinewood Road (the same place as USLN, USGS 0204295505): water levels inferred by machine learning from the USGS webcam images (the model retrained after the September 2026 nor'easter, live since 2026-10-08 9:12 PM EDT), archived every 6 hours in monthly files obs/floodvision/USLN-X_YYYY-MM.csv, with USLN's harmonic tide, datums and P-ETSS surge.

CNRC (Accokeek Creek at Crow's Nest Research Center) is the VIMS sensor on GreenStream's Virginia Flood Monitoring System (VDEM), archived every 6 hours in monthly files obs/greenstream/CNRC_YYYY-MM.csv. Its tide is fitted to that archive (13 constituents until it holds 6 months, then 27), with Dahlgren's P-ETSS surge and NAVD88 from NOAA VDatum (0.69 ft above MLLW).

Stand-ins: ALEV, ARPV, AXTV, BBIV, BPAD, CCHV, CHIV, CRSM, DPCV, ELGV, EZMV, FEDV, GNTD, GTND, HPPV, IHDM, JLMV, JPHV, KMNV, LCEV, LENM, LSPV, NRSV, ONAV, PCCV, PRTV, RAPV, RDIV, RICV, SAXV, SGSM, SLIM, SLRV, SMSV, VIMS, WCKV, WRDV, WRSV, WRVV, WSPV are not on Tidewatch yet (their VIMS pages link here), so until Tidewatch publishes them they are built the same way from the NWS NWPS gauge observations (VDEM, StormSense, USGS, county and NOAA gauges; GNTD repeats GTND, Georgetown): the gauge datum moved to MLLW (details in sources.csv), a harmonic tide fitted to the record, and the P-ETSS surge of the nearest P-ETSS point. Each steps aside as soon as Tidewatch publishes the station.

Charts built here (rather than copied from Tidewatch) carry an ⓘ after the title: click the title to show their data sources. sources.csv lists the same for every such station.
